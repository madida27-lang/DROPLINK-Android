DROPLINK Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 September 2026

DROPLINK is operated by Deutronoma Group in South Africa. This policy explains how personal information is processed when customers, drivers and dispatch/management use DROPLINK.

Information we process

Depending on how you use DROPLINK, we may process your name, phone number, optional email address, pickup and drop-off addresses, sender/receiver details, package and delivery information, delivery-related messages, driver location while the Driver app is in use, and proof-of-delivery photos, recipient names and notes.

Why we use information

We use this information to create, assign and complete deliveries; coordinate customers and drivers; provide navigation and delivery status; support trip communication; verify delivery completion; maintain security; prevent abuse; resolve disputes; and comply with applicable legal obligations.

Driver location

The DROPLINK Driver app may process precise or approximate location while the app is in use so dispatch can coordinate deliveries and delivery progress can be provided. The current production source is designed not to request Android background-location permission.

Proof-of-delivery photos

Approved drivers may upload delivery photos for verification and support. Drivers should avoid capturing unnecessary personal information in delivery photos.

Sharing

Relevant delivery information may be shared with the customer, assigned driver, dispatch/management, hosting or technical service providers needed to operate DROPLINK, and authorities where required by law. We do not sell personal information.

Security and retention

We use HTTPS, authenticated accounts, role-based access and password hashing. Records are retained for as long as reasonably necessary for delivery operations, support, disputes, fraud prevention and legal obligations, after which they should be deleted or de-identified where appropriate.

Your rights and account/data requests

Subject to applicable law, including South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA), you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of eligible personal information. Some records may need to be retained where required for legal or legitimate operational reasons.
